Privacy

What the board shows. What it hides.

Public

Username, lot description, current bid, and timer. That is all a buyer or seller sees on another desk.

Hidden

Legal name, street address, phone, and email stay in the clerk file. They are not printed on lots, bids, or profiles. They are not sold or handed to a third-party advertiser or data broker.

Password

Every buyer and every seller must set their own password. Eight characters, with a letter and a number. We store a hash, not the password itself.

IP address

We do not store your IP on the account and we do not show it to other users. Network logs on the host are not part of the public board.

Email confirm

We use your email only to confirm the desk and to reach you about your own lots or bids. That address is not listed next to a lot.
